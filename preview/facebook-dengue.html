Orange County is now under a Mosquito-borne Illness Alert. The Florida Department of Health confirmed three locally transmitted cases of Dengue Fever in Orange County. Across Florida, more than 150 locally acquired dengue cases have been confirmed in 2026, making this the most active mosquito-borne illness season in recent memory. Dengue is spread through the bite of infected mosquitoes, primarily the Aedes aegypti species. These are the same mosquitoes that breed in standing water in your yard: gutters, plant trays, bird baths, tarps, tires, anything holding even a small amount of water. They bite during the day and they are aggressive. Symptoms can include high fever, severe headache, eye pain, and intense muscle and joint pain. It is sometimes called "breakbone fever." There is no treatment other than supportive care. The only protection is reducing exposure.
