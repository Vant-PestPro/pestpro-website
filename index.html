Our Story

Florida's Premier Pest Control — Since 1988

Pest Pro was founded in 1988 by Dr. Bernie L. Howell, PhD, a renowned entomologist who built this company on one principle: understand the science of pests before you try to eliminate them. Dr. Howell continues to operate the original South Florida office and serves as the accredited entomologist behind every Pest Pro treatment.

In 1998, Daniel Rumsey, CPO entered the pest control industry and joined Pest Pro. In January 2020, Daniel assumed leadership of the Central Florida branch, expanding operations across Orange, Osceola, Lake, Polk, and Seminole counties. Today Pest Pro serves hundreds of residential and commercial clients backed by nearly four decades of company-wide expertise. FDACS License: JB304313.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does pest control cost in Central Florida?

Pest Pro's general maintenance plans start at $33/month (billed at $99/visit quarterly). Bi-monthly service is $79/visit. Initial infestation treatments are quoted after a free inspection — call us or fill out the form.

Do you offer same-day pest control service?

Yes. Same-day service is available in most Central Florida service areas. Our phones are answered 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Call (407) 922-2276 to schedule.

Are your treatments safe for pets and children?

Yes. We use Integrated Pest Management (IPM) protocols that prioritize low-impact, targeted treatments. We use products approved for residential use and will advise you on any precautions needed.

What areas do you serve in Central Florida?

We serve Orange, Osceola, Lake, Polk, and Seminole counties — including Orlando, Kissimmee, Clermont, Winter Garden, Windermere, St. Cloud, Sanford, Lakeland, and surrounding areas.

Do you offer commercial pest control?

Yes. We specialize in commercial pest management for restaurants, hotels, resorts, timeshares, healthcare facilities, warehouses, office buildings, and more — with full compliance documentation.

How do I know if I have a German roach infestation?

Signs include small dark droppings, egg casings, a musty odor, and roaches visible during the day. If you're seeing them in daylight, the infestation is already significant. Call us immediately.
