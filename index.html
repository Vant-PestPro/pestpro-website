Our Story

Florida's Premier Pest Control — Since 1986

What started as a single operation in South Florida in 1986 has grown into one of Central Florida's most trusted names in pest control. Our founder, Dr. Bernie L. Howell, PhD, built this company on one principle: understand the science of pests before you try to eliminate them.

In 2017, Daniel Rumsey — who has worked with Dr. Howell and Pest Pro since 1996 — stepped in to lead the Orlando division when the previous operator retired. In January 2020, Pest Pro LLC became fully independent, carrying forward the same science-backed standards, family values, and commitment to results that have defined this brand for nearly four decades.

Science-Backed Integrated Pest Management (IPM)

We don't guess. We analyze pest biology, behavior, and environmental factors to deliver targeted, long-lasting solutions with fewer treatments.

Family Owned & Operated
