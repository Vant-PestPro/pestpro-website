Our Story

Florida's Premier Pest Control Since 1988

Pest Pro was founded in 1988 by Dr. Bernie L. Howell, PhD, a renowned entomologist who built this company on one principle: understand the science of pests before you try to eliminate them.

In 1998, Daniel Rumsey, CPO entered the pest control industry and joined Pest Pro. In January 2020, Daniel assumed leadership of the Central Florida branch, serving hundreds of residential and commercial clients backed by nearly four decades of company-wide expertise. FDACS License: JB304313.
