Rooted in experience since 1986

Dr. Bernie L. Howell, PhD founded Pest Pro Services, LLC in South Florida in 1986. Daniel Rumsey took the helm of the Orlando division in 2017, and Pest Pro LLC became fully independent in January 2020 — carrying forward the same science-backed standards and family values.
