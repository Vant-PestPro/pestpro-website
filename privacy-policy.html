Privacy Policy

How Pest Pro LLC collects, uses, and protects your information.

Last updated: April 20, 2026

Who We Are

Pest Pro LLC is a licensed pest control company serving Central Florida. Our address is 3211 Vineland Road, Suite 107, Kissimmee, FL 34746. You can reach us at (407) 922-2276 or info@pestprollc.com.

This policy explains what information we collect when you contact us or use our website, how we use it, and what choices you have.

What Information We Collect

We only collect information you give us directly. This includes:

  • Contact details, your name, phone number, and email address when you fill out our contact or inspection request form.
  • Service details, information about the pest issue you're dealing with or the type of property you're inquiring about.
  • Communications, any messages you send us by phone, email, or through our website form.

We do not collect payment information through this website. We do not use tracking cookies, third-party advertising pixels, or behavioral analytics tools.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide for one purpose: to respond to your inquiry and provide pest control services. Specifically, we use it to:

  • Contact you to schedule a free inspection or follow up on your request
  • Communicate about your service appointments, treatment details, and follow-up visits
  • Send service documentation, invoices, or reports related to work we've performed

We do not use your information for marketing campaigns, third-party promotions, or any purpose unrelated to your service with Pest Pro LLC.

Phone, SMS, and Email Communication

When you submit a form on our website, you agree to receive follow-up contact from Pest Pro LLC by phone, SMS text message, or email. This contact is limited to scheduling your inspection, confirming appointments, and communicating about your service.

You can opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. To opt out of email communications, reply to any email from us and ask to be removed. To stop all contact, call us at (407) 922-2276.

Do We Share Your Information?

No. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with any third parties for their own purposes. The only people who have access to your information are Pest Pro LLC staff involved in scheduling and providing your service.

We use Formspree to process form submissions on this website. Formspree receives your form data and delivers it to us by email. Their privacy policy is available at formspree.io.

How Long We Keep Your Information

We keep your contact information for as long as you are an active customer or as long as needed to provide our services. If you have not received service from us and request that we delete your information, we will do so promptly.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Ask what information we have about you
  • Request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Request that we delete your information
  • Opt out of any communications from us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@pestprollc.com or call (407) 922-2276. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect the information you share with us. Our website uses HTTPS encryption. We do not store your form submissions on our website, they are delivered directly to our team by email.

Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, we will post the revised version here with an updated date. We encourage you to review it periodically. Continued use of our website after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your information, reach out directly:

  • Email: info@pestprollc.com
