Stinging Insects in Central Florida

Central Florida's warm climate supports a variety of stinging insects year-round. While most bees and wasps are beneficial to the environment, nests on or near your home or business pose a serious risk , especially for children, pets, and anyone with allergies.

Never attempt to remove an active nest yourself. Disturbing a nest without proper equipment and technique can trigger a defensive swarm. Pest Pro's technicians are trained to safely eliminate and remove nests with minimal risk.

Common Species We Handle

  • Yellow Jackets . Aggressive, often nest in ground or wall voids. Sting repeatedly.
  • Paper Wasps . Build open, umbrella-shaped nests under eaves, porch ceilings, and railings.
  • Mud Daubers . Build mud tube nests. Generally non-aggressive but unwanted.
  • Bald-Faced Hornets . Large aerial nests, highly aggressive when disturbed.
  • Carpenter Bees . Bore into wood structures, causing damage over time.
  • Honey Bees . We treat established colonies; live honeybee removal/relocation is handled as needed.

Commercial Stinging Insect Programs

Hotels, resorts, restaurants, and event venues with outdoor spaces cannot have wasp or bee nests near guests. We provide rapid response and scheduled inspection programs for commercial properties across Central Florida.
