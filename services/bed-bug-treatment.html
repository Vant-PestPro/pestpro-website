The Central Florida Bed Bug Problem

Central Florida welcomes over 75 million visitors every year, more than any other tourist destination in the world. That volume of travel creates a constant cycle of bed bug introduction across the region's hotels, resorts, timeshares, vacation rentals, and private residences.

Bed bugs don't discriminate. They're found in luxury resorts and budget motels alike. They travel in luggage, on clothing, and in secondhand furniture. Once introduced, they spread quickly between rooms, units, and floors, and the longer they go undetected, the more expensive and complex the treatment becomes.

Pest Pro's dedicated bed bug team has been treating properties across Central Florida for years. We understand the urgency, the need for discretion, and the compliance requirements that hotels and commercial properties face. When you need it done right, quietly and completely, we're the team to call.

Bed Bugs Spread Fast. Don't Wait

A single female bed bug can lay 200–500 eggs in her lifetime. An untreated room can become an untreated floor within weeks. If you suspect bed bugs, call us immediately at (407) 922-2276.

Signs You Have Bed Bugs

  • Small rust-colored or dark spots on mattresses, sheets, or walls (fecal matter)
  • Tiny white eggs or shed skins in mattress seams and crevices
  • Bites in a line or cluster pattern on skin (arms, neck, legs)
  • Live insects, flat, oval, reddish-brown, about the size of an apple seed
  • A musty, sweet odor in heavily infested rooms
