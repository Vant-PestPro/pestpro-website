The Window Between Early Activity and Full Infestation Is Narrow

Food service environments inherently provide everything a pest population needs to establish and grow. A small, unaddressed problem in a kitchen, dry storage, or receiving area can escalate faster than most operators expect. The businesses that stay clean and stay open are the ones with a dedicated, proactive pest management program already in place before there is ever a visible issue.

Our Approach

A Dedicated, Highly Skilled Team. Every Visit.

You must have a highly skilled, dedicated team regularly inspecting and implementing your Integrated Pest Management system. A rotating spray schedule is not an IPM program. A real program means understanding where pests come from, what conditions sustain them, and how to eliminate those conditions before they create an active problem.

Pest Pro builds custom IPM programs for food service environments that include scheduled inspections, targeted treatment of identified harborage areas, pheromone and glue monitoring stations, structural consultation, and full health department-ready documentation.

Scheduled Inspection and Monitoring

Regular visits timed around your operating hours. We inspect all high-risk areas: behind and beneath equipment, grease traps, floor drains, dry storage, receiving areas, and utility chases. Monitoring stations placed at key harborage and entry points.

Targeted German Roach and Rodent Protocols

German roaches require gel baiting and crack-and-crevice application in harborage zones, not broadcast spraying. Rodent programs combine tamper-resistant bait stations, snap traps in non-food areas, and structural exclusion consultation to eliminate entry points.

Health Department Ready Documentation

Every inspection and treatment is documented with date, technician, areas treated, products applied, and findings noted. Reports are formatted for health department review. If an inspector walks in tomorrow, you are ready.

Sanitation and Structural Advisory

The best pesticide is a clean, sealed facility. We identify and document conditions contributing to pest pressure: open drains, standing water, gaps in walls and equipment, improperly stored food, and waste management deficiencies. Fixing these conditions is the foundation of a real IPM program.

Rapid Response for Active Events

When something is found between scheduled visits, we respond quickly. Typically within 24 hours. Emergency response is available by calling us directly. Pest activity in a food service environment cannot wait.

Third-Party Audit Compliance Programs

Many food service operations are subject to third-party food safety audits, including SQF, AIB, BRC, and similar standards that require a documented, verifiable pest management program. Pest Pro builds and maintains IPM programs specifically structured to meet third-party audit criteria. All inspection reports, treatment logs, monitoring records, and corrective actions are audit-ready and available on request. We work directly with your quality assurance team to ensure your pest control documentation holds up to any auditor walking through the door.
