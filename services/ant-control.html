Florida Ants Are a Year-Round Problem

Central Florida's warm, humid climate makes it a year-round haven for ants. With dozens of species present in the region, effective ant control starts with correct identification. Ghost ants in your kitchen require a completely different treatment than fire ant mounds in your yard , treating the wrong species the wrong way only makes the problem worse.

Pest Pro's technicians are trained to identify ant species on sight and deploy targeted treatments that eliminate colonies at the source, not just the ants you can see.

Common Ant Species We Treat

  • Ghost Ants . Tiny, pale ants common in kitchens and bathrooms. Trails follow moisture sources.
  • Fire Ants . Aggressive, stinging ants that build large mounds. A serious hazard for children and pets.
  • Carpenter Ants . Large, wood-boring ants that can damage structures over time.
  • Bigheaded Ants . Common in lawns and landscaping, often mistaken for fire ants.
  • Argentine Ants . Form massive super-colonies with multiple queens. Difficult to control without professional treatment.
  • Pavement Ants . Nest under concrete slabs, driveways, and foundations.

Our Ant Treatment Approach

We use Integrated Pest Management (IPM) principles to address ant problems permanently. That means finding and treating the colony, not just the trail. Our treatment may include targeted baits, perimeter treatments, void injections, and exclusion recommendations to prevent re-entry.

1

Species Identification

Different ants, different solutions. We identify exactly what you're dealing with before treatment begins.

2

Colony Location

We trace trails to find entry points and nesting sites , inside walls, under slabs, in landscaping.

3

Targeted Treatment

Species-appropriate baits and treatments applied directly to colonies and entry points.

4

Prevention

Seal entry points, eliminate moisture and food sources, and schedule follow-up to confirm elimination.
